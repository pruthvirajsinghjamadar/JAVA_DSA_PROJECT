Your certificates

Pass a topic quiz and solve its coding challenges to earn a topic certificate. Finish every topic in a level for a level certificate, and create the full course certificate once all 24 are done.

How certificates work

  • Topic certificate: pass the quiz (at least 4 of 5 correct) and solve at least 3 of its 4 coding challenges.
  • Level certificate: earn the topic certificate in every topic of that level. It appears on its own.
  • Full course certificate: earn all 24 topic certificates, then type your name and create it.
